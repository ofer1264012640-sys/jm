José Mourinho at FC Porto

José Mourinho returned to FC Porto in January 2002, taking over a team that was going through a difficult period. Porto were fifth in the Portuguese league when Mourinho arrived, seven points behind the leaders.

Mourinho immediately accepted the challenge. His arrival marked the beginning of one of the most extraordinary managerial periods in FC Porto's history.

What initially looked like a difficult mid-season appointment quickly developed into the foundation of a European champion.

Turning Porto Around

Mourinho inherited a team that was struggling to keep pace at the top of the Portuguese league. Instead of simply trying to stabilise the situation, he quickly imposed his own tactical ideas and demanded a much higher level of organisation from his players.

Porto improved dramatically under Mourinho. His side became extremely difficult to beat and combined defensive organisation with fast and intelligent attacking football.

Mourinho also developed a strong relationship with his players. Several members of the squad became central figures in the greatest successes of his Porto era, including Deco, Ricardo Carvalho, Paulo Ferreira, Maniche and Costinha.

2002–03: The Breakthrough Season

Mourinho's first full season at Porto was extraordinary. The club won the Portuguese league and the Portuguese Cup, completing a domestic double.

But the biggest achievement came in Europe. Porto reached the UEFA Cup final in Seville, where they faced Celtic in a dramatic match.

Porto won 3–2 after extra time, with Derlei scoring the decisive goal. Mourinho had won his first major European trophy as a manager and announced himself on the European stage.

The UEFA Cup triumph was particularly significant because Porto had not simply dominated domestically. Mourinho had taken the club to European success while building a team that would become even stronger the following season.

2003–04: The Greatest Achievement

The following season would become the defining chapter of Mourinho's first spell at Porto.

Porto successfully defended their Portuguese league title and once again established themselves as the strongest team in the country.

In Europe, Mourinho's team progressed through the UEFA Champions League and eventually reached the final in Gelsenkirchen.

In the final, Porto faced AS Monaco. Mourinho's side produced a dominant performance and won 3–0, with goals from Carlos Alberto, Deco and Dmitriy Alenichev.

FC Porto were European champions.

The victory completed one of the greatest European stories in modern football. Just two years after Mourinho had arrived at Porto, he had transformed the club into champions of Europe.

The Manchester United Comeback

One of the most memorable moments of Mourinho's Porto campaign came in the Champions League round of 16 against Manchester United.

Porto appeared to be heading out after Manchester United took the lead in the tie. However, Mourinho's team recovered and turned the tie around.

Porto eventually eliminated Manchester United and continued their journey toward the Champions League final.

European Champions

Porto entered the 2004 Champions League final as underdogs against AS Monaco. Mourinho's tactical organisation and Porto's disciplined performance produced a convincing 3–0 victory.

The Champions League triumph confirmed Mourinho as one of the most important young managers in European football.

Legacy

Mourinho left FC Porto in the summer of 2004 after winning the Champions League. He departed with his reputation completely transformed.

In just over two years, Mourinho had gone from taking over a team in a difficult position to winning major domestic and European trophies, including the Champions League.

His success at Porto became the foundation for the next stage of his career at Chelsea and established Mourinho as one of the most sought-after managers in world football.
