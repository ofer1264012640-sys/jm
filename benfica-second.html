Mourinho Returns to Benfica

José Mourinho returned to Benfica in 2025 for his second spell at the Portuguese club.

More than two decades after his first experience at Benfica, Mourinho returned to the club with a wealth of experience from some of the biggest clubs in European football.

His return generated enormous attention in Portugal and among Benfica supporters.

Back in Portuguese Football

Mourinho returned to Portuguese football after years of managing at the highest level in England, Italy, Spain and Turkey.

His objective was to compete for domestic success while also making Benfica competitive in Europe.

His extensive experience allowed him to bring a different level of tactical knowledge and international experience to the squad.

Mourinho's Tactical Approach

Mourinho continued to rely on defensive organisation, tactical discipline and quick transitions.

His teams were expected to remain compact without the ball and attack quickly after winning possession.

At Benfica, Mourinho also had to balance his tactical approach with the club's traditional expectation of controlling matches and competing for titles.

European Ambitions

Benfica's European ambitions were an important part of Mourinho's return to Portugal.

Mourinho had already won major European trophies throughout his career and brought that experience into another European campaign.

Mourinho's Second Benfica Era

Mourinho's second spell at Benfica represented a remarkable return to one of the clubs where his managerial career had begun.

His return also added another chapter to a career that had already taken him through some of the biggest clubs in Europe.

The End of the Second Benfica Era

Mourinho's second spell at Benfica added another chapter to his long managerial career.

After returning to Portugal more than twenty years after his first spell at the club, Mourinho once again managed Benfica at the highest level.

His second Benfica spell ended after 45 matches, with 27 victories, 10 draws and 8 defeats.
