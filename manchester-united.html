Mourinho Arrives at Manchester United

In the summer of 2016, José Mourinho was appointed manager of Manchester United, taking charge of one of the biggest clubs in English football.

Mourinho arrived at Old Trafford with a reputation as one of the most successful managers in modern football.

Manchester United had gone through several difficult seasons since the retirement of Sir Alex Ferguson, and Mourinho was expected to return the club to the top of English and European football.

His appointment created enormous expectations among Manchester United supporters.

Building the Squad

Mourinho immediately strengthened the squad with several major signings.

Zlatan Ibrahimović arrived from Paris Saint-Germain, while Paul Pogba returned to Manchester United from Juventus.

Henrikh Mkhitaryan also joined the club, while Eric Bailly became an important addition to the defence.

Mourinho was building a team with a mixture of experience, physical strength and attacking quality.

2016–17: The First Season

Mourinho's first season at Manchester United produced several trophies.

United competed in the Premier League while also taking part in the Europa League and domestic cup competitions.

The team finished sixth in the Premier League, but Mourinho's season was defined by success in the cup competitions.

Community Shield

Mourinho's first competitive trophy with Manchester United came in the Community Shield.

United faced Leicester City at Wembley.

Zlatan Ibrahimović scored the decisive goal as Manchester United won 2–1.

Mourinho had immediately added silverware to his Manchester United career.

League Cup

Manchester United also reached the League Cup final during Mourinho's first season.

They faced Southampton in the final at Wembley.

Manchester United won the match 3–2 in a dramatic final.

Zlatan Ibrahimović scored twice and Jesse Lingard also found the net.

Mourinho had won another trophy in his first season at Old Trafford.

UEFA Europa League

The Europa League became the biggest European target of Mourinho's first season.

Manchester United progressed through the knockout stages and reached the final in Stockholm.

They faced Ajax in the final.

Goals from Paul Pogba and Henrikh Mkhitaryan gave Manchester United a 2–0 victory.

Mourinho had won the UEFA Europa League and secured Champions League qualification for the following season.

2017–18: Finishing Second

Mourinho's second season began with Manchester United looking much stronger in the Premier League.

The team challenged for the title for much of the campaign.

Manchester United eventually finished second in the Premier League.

It was their highest league finish since Sir Alex Ferguson's retirement.

The team also reached the FA Cup final.

However, Manchester United lost the final to Chelsea.

Champions League

Manchester United returned to the Champions League under Mourinho during the 2017–18 season.

They progressed from the group stage but were eliminated by Sevilla in the Round of 16.

The European campaign ended earlier than Mourinho and the club had hoped.

Mourinho's Football at Manchester United

Mourinho's Manchester United teams were built around defensive organisation, physical strength and quick transitions.

Players such as Paul Pogba, Nemanja Matić and Ander Herrera provided strength and control in midfield.

Zlatan Ibrahimović provided experience and goals during the first season, while Romelu Lukaku became the main striker during the second campaign.

The defence was led by players including Eric Bailly, Chris Smalling and Antonio Valencia.

Mourinho's Legacy at Manchester United

Mourinho's time at Manchester United produced three trophies and a second-place Premier League finish.

His first season was particularly successful, with United winning the Community Shield, League Cup and Europa League.

Mourinho also restored Manchester United to the Champions League and helped the club achieve its highest Premier League finish since Ferguson's retirement.

Leaving Manchester United

Mourinho's third season began with increasing pressure and inconsistent results.

In December 2018, Manchester United announced that Mourinho had left the club.

His Manchester United career had come to an end after two and a half seasons.

The End of the Manchester United Era

José Mourinho left Manchester United in December 2018 after two and a half seasons at Old Trafford.

Despite the difficult ending, he had delivered three trophies and guided the club back into the Champions League.

His spell at Manchester United remains an important chapter in Mourinho's managerial career.
