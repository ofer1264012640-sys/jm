Mourinho Arrives at Tottenham

In November 2019, José Mourinho was appointed manager of Tottenham Hotspur following the departure of Mauricio Pochettino.

Mourinho returned to the Premier League with the challenge of taking Tottenham to the next level and competing for major trophies.

Tottenham had reached the UEFA Champions League final only months earlier, and Mourinho inherited a talented squad containing players such as Harry Kane, Son Heung-min, Christian Eriksen and Dele Alli.

2019–20: The First Season

Mourinho began his Tottenham career with a strong start, winning several of his opening matches.

His team relied heavily on the attacking partnership of Harry Kane and Son Heung-min.

Tottenham also had to deal with several injuries and a difficult Premier League campaign.

Despite the challenges, Mourinho guided Tottenham to a sixth-place finish in the Premier League and qualification for the Europa League.

2020–21: A Promising Start

Tottenham began the 2020–21 season strongly and quickly moved towards the top of the Premier League.

Mourinho's side became known for its dangerous counter-attacking football.

Son Heung-min and Harry Kane formed one of the most productive attacking partnerships in Europe.

Tottenham also reached the final of the League Cup, giving Mourinho an opportunity to win his first trophy with the club.

Kane & Son

One of the defining features of Mourinho's Tottenham was the relationship between Harry Kane and Son Heung-min.

The two attackers regularly combined for goals and assists and became one of the most effective attacking partnerships in the Premier League.

Mourinho frequently used quick transitions to take advantage of their pace, movement and finishing.

Europa League

Tottenham competed in the UEFA Europa League during the 2020–21 season.

The team progressed through the early stages but suffered a dramatic elimination against Dinamo Zagreb in the Round of 16.

Tottenham had won the first leg 2–0, but lost the second leg 3–0 after extra time and were eliminated.

League Cup Final

Tottenham reached the League Cup final during the 2020–21 season.

However, Mourinho was dismissed before the final, meaning he did not get the opportunity to manage Tottenham in the final.

Mourinho's Tactical Approach

Mourinho's Tottenham often relied on defensive organisation and fast attacking transitions.

The team could defend in a compact shape before quickly attacking the space behind the opposition defence.

Harry Kane often dropped deeper to participate in build-up play, while Son Heung-min attacked space behind the defensive line.

This combination produced many goals and memorable attacking performances.

Leaving Tottenham

On 19 April 2021, Tottenham announced that Mourinho had left the club.

His departure came just days before the League Cup final.

Mourinho's Tottenham career had ended without a major trophy, despite reaching the League Cup final.

Mourinho's Legacy at Tottenham

Mourinho's time at Tottenham was one of the more complicated periods of his managerial career.

He produced some impressive attacking football, particularly through the partnership between Kane and Son.

However, the team struggled for consistency and ultimately failed to win a trophy during his tenure.

The End of the Tottenham Era

José Mourinho's time at Tottenham ended in April 2021, after approximately seventeen months in charge.

Although he did not win a trophy with the club, his period included memorable performances and the emergence of one of Europe's most dangerous attacking partnerships in Harry Kane and Son Heung-min.
