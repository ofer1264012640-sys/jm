Mourinho Returns to Chelsea

After three seasons at Real Madrid, José Mourinho returned to Chelsea in the summer of 2013 for his second spell at the club.

Mourinho had already become a club legend during his first spell at Stamford Bridge, winning two Premier League titles and establishing Chelsea as one of the strongest teams in England.

His return was met with enormous excitement from Chelsea supporters, who welcomed back the manager they had nicknamed "The Special One".

Mourinho returned with the goal of building another successful Chelsea team and competing for trophies in England and Europe.

2013–14: The Return

Mourinho's first season back at Chelsea was a period of rebuilding.

Chelsea had a talented squad containing players such as John Terry, Frank Lampard, Petr Čech and Eden Hazard.

Mourinho also began developing a younger generation of players who would become important to Chelsea's future.

Chelsea competed strongly in the Premier League and eventually finished third.

In the Champions League, Chelsea reached the semi-finals before being eliminated by Atlético Madrid.

Although Mourinho did not win a major trophy during the season, Chelsea showed clear signs of progress.

Building the New Chelsea

During the summer of 2014, Chelsea made several important additions to the squad.

Diego Costa arrived from Atlético Madrid and quickly became the team's main striker.

Cesc Fàbregas joined from Barcelona and became one of the team's most important creative players.

Thibaut Courtois also returned to the club after his successful loan spell at Atlético Madrid.

Mourinho had assembled a team combining experienced leaders with talented young players.

2014–15: The Championship Season

Chelsea began the 2014–15 season extremely strongly.

The team quickly established itself at the top of the Premier League and remained there for much of the campaign.

Chelsea's football was built around defensive organisation, midfield control and the attacking quality of players such as Eden Hazard, Diego Costa and Cesc Fàbregas.

John Terry remained the leader of the defence, while Nemanja Matić provided strength and balance in midfield.

League Cup

Chelsea reached the League Cup final and faced Tottenham Hotspur at Wembley.

Chelsea won the final 2–0.

John Terry and Diego Costa were among the key figures in the victory.

Mourinho had won his first trophy since returning to Chelsea.

Premier League Champions

Chelsea continued their excellent league campaign throughout the season.

The team eventually secured the Premier League title with several matches still remaining.

It was Mourinho's third Premier League title with Chelsea and his first since returning to the club.

Chelsea finished the season as English champions.

Eden Hazard

One of the defining players of Mourinho's second Chelsea spell was Eden Hazard.

The Belgian winger developed into one of the best attacking players in the Premier League.

Hazard's ability to carry the ball, create chances and score important goals made him central to Mourinho's Chelsea team.

In 2014–15, Hazard was named the PFA Players' Player of the Year.

Mourinho's Tactical Approach

Mourinho's second Chelsea team was known for its tactical discipline and defensive organisation.

Chelsea were difficult to break down and extremely effective at controlling matches.

The midfield partnership of Cesc Fàbregas and Nemanja Matić provided creativity and defensive balance.

Meanwhile, John Terry and Gary Cahill formed the foundation of a strong defensive unit.

Chelsea could also quickly transition from defence to attack through Hazard, Willian, Oscar and Diego Costa.

The End of the Second Spell

The 2014–15 season ended with Chelsea as Premier League champions and League Cup winners.

Mourinho had successfully returned Chelsea to the top of English football.

However, the following season proved extremely difficult.

Chelsea struggled for consistency and Mourinho's second spell eventually came to an end in December 2015.

Despite the difficult ending, Mourinho had returned to Stamford Bridge and delivered another Premier League title.

Mourinho's Legacy at Chelsea

Mourinho's second spell at Chelsea added another Premier League title and another League Cup to his collection.

His return demonstrated that he could once again build a championship-winning team at Stamford Bridge.

The 2014–15 Chelsea side remains one of the most successful teams of Mourinho's later career.

The End of an Era

José Mourinho's second spell at Chelsea ended in December 2015.

Although the final months were difficult, his return had produced another Premier League title and another domestic cup.

Mourinho left Chelsea having won three Premier League titles and three League Cups across his two spells at the club.
