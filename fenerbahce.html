Mourinho Arrives at Fenerbahçe

In June 2024, José Mourinho was appointed manager of Fenerbahçe, returning to management after his spell at Roma.

His arrival created enormous excitement in Turkey, with Fenerbahçe supporters expecting Mourinho to challenge for domestic honours and make an impact in European competition.

Mourinho arrived with extensive experience from some of the biggest clubs in European football, including Porto, Chelsea, Inter Milan, Real Madrid, Manchester United and Roma.

The Süper Lig Challenge

Fenerbahçe entered the 2024–25 season with the goal of winning the Turkish Süper Lig.

Mourinho's team competed at the top of the table and attempted to end the club's long wait for a league championship.

The season also featured a fierce rivalry with Galatasaray, making the title race particularly competitive.

European Competition

Fenerbahçe also competed in UEFA competitions during Mourinho's first season in Turkey.

Mourinho's European experience was an important part of the club's ambitions.

Fenerbahçe looked to establish itself as a competitive force in European football while simultaneously fighting for domestic success.

Mourinho's Tactical Approach

Mourinho continued to rely on many of the principles that had defined his career.

Defensive organisation, tactical discipline and quick transitions remained important elements of his approach.

Fenerbahçe's squad contained experienced players who could adapt to Mourinho's tactical demands.

Mourinho and the Fenerbahçe Fans

Mourinho's arrival generated enormous attention among Fenerbahçe supporters.

His reputation and personality made him one of the biggest managerial names ever to work in Turkish football.

The appointment also attracted significant international attention to the Turkish Süper Lig.

Leaving Fenerbahçe

Mourinho's time at Fenerbahçe ended in August 2025 following the club's elimination from the Champions League qualifying rounds.

His departure brought an end to his first managerial experience in Turkish football.

Mourinho's Legacy at Fenerbahçe

Mourinho's arrival represented one of the biggest managerial appointments in the history of Turkish football.

His presence brought worldwide attention to Fenerbahçe and the Turkish Süper Lig.

Although his spell was relatively short, it added another country and another major football league to Mourinho's extraordinary managerial career.
