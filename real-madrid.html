José Mourinho at Real Madrid

After completing an historic treble with Inter Milan in the 2009–10 season, José Mourinho accepted one of the biggest challenges of his managerial career: becoming manager of Real Madrid.

Real Madrid were searching for a manager capable of competing with Barcelona, who were dominating Spanish football under Pep Guardiola.

Mourinho arrived in Madrid with an extraordinary reputation. He had already won league titles in Portugal, England and Italy, and had won the UEFA Champions League twice.

His appointment created enormous expectations at the Santiago Bernabéu.

2010–11: The First Season

Mourinho's first season at Real Madrid was immediately defined by the rivalry with Barcelona.

Real Madrid had a talented squad containing players such as Iker Casillas, Sergio Ramos, Xabi Alonso, Cristiano Ronaldo and Karim Benzema.

Mourinho also worked with young players who would become important figures in the future, including Mesut Özil and Ángel Di María.

Real Madrid competed strongly in La Liga, but Barcelona eventually finished first.

Copa del Rey

The biggest success of Mourinho's first season came in the Copa del Rey.

Real Madrid reached the final and faced Barcelona at Mestalla in Valencia.

The match remained goalless after 90 minutes.

In extra time, Cristiano Ronaldo scored the decisive goal with a powerful header.

Real Madrid won 1–0 and Mourinho lifted his first trophy as Real Madrid manager.

Champions League Semi-Final

Real Madrid also reached the Champions League semi-finals during Mourinho's first season.

They faced Barcelona once again.

Barcelona won the tie and progressed to the final, ending Real Madrid's European campaign.

Despite the disappointment, Mourinho had restored Real Madrid to the latter stages of the Champions League.

2011–12: The Record-Breaking League Season

Mourinho's second season became one of the greatest league campaigns in Real Madrid's history.

Real Madrid attacked with incredible efficiency and scored goals at an extraordinary rate.

Cristiano Ronaldo was one of the central figures of the team, while players such as Mesut Özil, Karim Benzema, Ángel Di María and Gonzalo Higuaín provided further attacking quality.

Real Madrid eventually won La Liga with a record 100 points.

They scored 121 goals during the campaign, setting a remarkable record for the club at the time.

Mourinho had defeated Barcelona in the league title race and delivered the Spanish championship to Real Madrid.

The El Clásico Rivalry

Mourinho's time at Real Madrid was closely connected to the rivalry with Barcelona.

The matches between the two clubs became some of the most watched and intense fixtures in world football.

Mourinho's tactical approach focused on limiting Barcelona's possession and exploiting spaces behind their defensive line.

His Real Madrid team combined defensive organisation with extremely fast transitions.

The rivalry produced dramatic matches, controversial moments and unforgettable battles between two of the greatest teams in the world.

Spanish Super Cup

At the beginning of the 2012–13 season, Real Madrid faced Barcelona in the Spanish Super Cup.

Real Madrid won the tie on aggregate and lifted the Spanish Super Cup.

Mourinho had added another trophy to his Real Madrid collection.

2012–13: The Final Season

Mourinho's third season was considerably more difficult.

Real Madrid struggled to maintain the same consistency they had shown during the record-breaking 2011–12 campaign.

The team finished second in La Liga behind Barcelona.

Despite the difficulties, Real Madrid once again reached the Champions League semi-finals.

They faced Borussia Dortmund but were eliminated after losing the first leg in Germany.

Mourinho's final season in Madrid ended without a major trophy.

Mourinho's Football at Madrid

Mourinho's Real Madrid was different from his Inter team.

In Madrid, he had players capable of playing extremely fast attacking football.

Cristiano Ronaldo provided enormous goal-scoring power, while Mesut Özil became one of the team's main creative players.

The team was particularly dangerous when winning the ball and attacking quickly.

Mourinho combined this attacking ability with the tactical discipline that had defined his teams at Porto, Chelsea and Inter.

Mourinho's Legacy at Real Madrid

Mourinho spent three seasons at Real Madrid and won three major trophies.

He won La Liga with a record 100 points, broke the club's scoring record in the competition and defeated Barcelona to claim the Spanish championship.

He also helped Real Madrid reach the Champions League semi-finals in three consecutive seasons.

His time in Madrid was defined by his rivalry with Barcelona and his battle against Pep Guardiola's historic team.

Leaving Real Madrid

In May 2013, Real Madrid announced that Mourinho would leave the club at the end of the season.

His three-year spell in Madrid had come to an end.

Mourinho left Spain having added another league title to his collection and having managed one of the biggest clubs in the world.

The End of the Madrid Era

José Mourinho's three seasons at Real Madrid were filled with trophies, records and some of the most intense rivalries in modern football.

He won La Liga with 100 points, broke the club's scoring record in the competition and defeated Barcelona to claim the Spanish championship.

Mourinho left Real Madrid in 2013 and returned to Chelsea for his second spell in England.
