Mourinho Arrives at Roma

In May 2021, José Mourinho was appointed manager of Roma, returning to Italy more than a decade after his historic spell with Inter Milan.

Mourinho arrived at the club with enormous expectations. Roma were looking to return to the highest level of Italian and European football, while Mourinho was determined to build another competitive team.

His appointment immediately created huge excitement among Roma supporters.

2021–22: A New Beginning

Mourinho's first season in Rome saw him introduce his tactical ideas and establish a strong connection with the supporters.

Roma competed in Serie A while also taking part in the newly created UEFA Europa Conference League.

The team progressed through the European competition and eventually reached the final.

UEFA Europa Conference League

Roma reached the final of the inaugural UEFA Europa Conference League in 2022.

They faced Feyenoord in the final in Tirana.

Nicolò Zaniolo scored the only goal of the match, giving Roma a 1–0 victory.

Mourinho became the first manager to win all three major UEFA club competitions.

The victory also gave Roma their first major European trophy.

2022–23: European Ambition

Mourinho's second season began with Roma looking to build on their European success.

The club continued to compete strongly in Serie A while also progressing in European competition.

Roma eventually reached another European final, this time in the UEFA Europa League.

Europa League Final

Roma faced Sevilla in the 2023 UEFA Europa League final in Budapest.

The match ended 1–1 after extra time and was decided by a penalty shootout.

Roma ultimately lost the shootout.

Despite the defeat, Mourinho had taken Roma to consecutive European finals.

2023–24: The Final Season

Mourinho began his third season at Roma with the objective of continuing the club's progress.

The team continued to compete in Serie A and European competition.

Roma remained one of the most competitive teams in Italian football and continued to develop young players alongside experienced leaders.

Mourinho's Tactical Approach

Mourinho's Roma was built around defensive organisation, physical strength and quick transitions.

The team frequently relied on a compact defensive structure before attacking quickly when possession was recovered.

Players such as Paulo Dybala, Lorenzo Pellegrini and Tammy Abraham played important roles in the attacking system.

Mourinho also placed significant importance on experienced defenders and midfielders who could maintain the team's tactical structure.

Mourinho and the Roma Supporters

Mourinho developed a particularly strong relationship with Roma supporters.

His personality and passion for the club made him extremely popular among many fans.

The Conference League triumph created one of the most memorable moments of his time in Rome.

Leaving Roma

Mourinho's time at Roma came to an end in January 2024.

Roma announced his departure after a difficult run of results.

His three-year spell had nevertheless produced a European trophy and another European final.

Mourinho's Legacy at Roma

Mourinho's Roma career will always be remembered for the UEFA Europa Conference League triumph in 2022.

The victory gave Roma their first major European trophy and made Mourinho the first manager to win the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League.

His team also reached the Europa League final one year later.

The End of the Roma Era

José Mourinho's time at Roma ended in January 2024, after almost three seasons in charge.

His biggest achievement was leading Roma to the inaugural UEFA Europa Conference League title.

Mourinho also guided Roma to the Europa League final in 2023, creating another memorable European run.
